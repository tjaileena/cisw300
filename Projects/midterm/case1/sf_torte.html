Red Velvet Cake Pops

by Jaileena T

A sweet bite-sized, red-velvet cake pop treat, perfect for celebrations, holidays, or just to enjoy!

Ingredients

  • 1 Red Velvet Cake Mix
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup vanilla frosting
  • 1 Bag of Milk Chocolate Candy Melts

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350° F (230° C).
  2. Mix cake mix, water, oil and eggs in large bowl.
  3. Use mixer or beat vigorously by hand for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour into pan.
  5. Bake for 28-33 min.
  6. Use toothpick, insert into center until it comes out clean.
  7. Let it cool.
  8. Once cooled down, crumble cake into a large bowl.
  9. Add vanilla frosting, then mix.
  10. Begin shaping into balls, until no cake leftover.
  11. Microwave candy melts for 25-30 sec, then mix until all melted.
  12. Use cake pop sticks, to hold each cake pop.
  13. Dip cake pop stick into candy melt, then into cake pop.
  14. Let that sit until stable (can hold cake pop up).
  15. Dip cake pop into candy melt.
  16. Decorate with additional candy melt or sprinkles.
  17. Let it cool on cake pop stand or pan with parchment paper.
  18. Repeat until all cake pops are covered.
